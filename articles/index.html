Debt Review: Clear Answers and Practical Guidance

Debt review is a legal process in South Africa for people who are struggling to keep up with their debt repayments. A registered debt counsellor reviews your finances and works with creditors on a structured repayment plan. These guides explain the basics; your options depend on your individual circumstances.

01

Debt Review Explained Simply

Debt review, also called debt counselling, is a formal process intended to help over-indebted consumers manage their debt repayments. A debt counsellor looks at your income, expenses, and debts to understand what may be affordable.

  • Your finances are assessed to build a picture of your current obligations and budget.
  • The counsellor can engage with your creditors to discuss a structured repayment plan.
  • A revised plan may make monthly repayments more manageable, but the terms and outcome depend on your circumstances.

Debt review may be worth discussing with a registered debt counsellor if your repayments have become difficult to manage. An individual assessment is needed to understand your options.

02

What Happens During Debt Review?

The process generally moves through a few main stages. The details and timing can vary depending on your financial situation and the discussions with creditors.

  1. Financial assessment: Your income, household expenses, and debt repayments are reviewed.
  2. Debt negotiation: Your debt counsellor engages creditors to discuss repayment options that fit the assessment.
  3. Repayment plan: If a plan is agreed, you follow the structured payment arrangement and keep track of your progress.
  4. Working towards completion: Continue following the plan and ask your counsellor what steps apply as you repay your debts.

Ask your counsellor to explain the proposed payment, how it will be distributed, and what to do if your circumstances change.

03

Debt Review: Common Questions Answered

Is debt review legal in South Africa?

Yes. Debt review is a legal process governed by the National Credit Act. A registered debt counsellor can explain how the process applies to your circumstances.

Can debt review affect my credit profile?

Debt review may affect your credit profile while you are under review. Ask your debt counsellor how your status is reported and what needs to happen before you can apply for further credit.

Can I take on new credit during debt review?

New credit may be restricted during the process. Discuss any questions about existing accounts or future credit with your debt counsellor before making a decision.

How long does the assessment take?

Timing varies. Assessment and approval may take a few weeks, but your own circumstances and creditor responses can affect the process. Ask your debt counsellor for an estimate based on your case.

Please note: This page provides general information, not personal financial or legal advice. Speak with a registered debt counsellor to discuss your situation and confirm which options may apply to you.
