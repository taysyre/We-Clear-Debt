About We Clear Debt

We help South Africans find a clearer way forward with practical guidance, professional support, and debt review solutions shaped around their circumstances.

Who we are

Real support, with people at the centre

We Clear Debt is a South African debt counselling provider helping families regain control of their finances. Our NCR-registered team offers guidance to people facing legal pressure, struggling to make ends meet, or looking for a fresh start.

We believe everyone deserves to understand their options. We take time to explain the process and help clients work towards a more manageable repayment plan.

Our mission

Help people move towards financial freedom with accessible debt review solutions, reduced monthly payments, and support through the process.

How we work

  • Put clients and their circumstances first
  • Explain options clearly and honestly
  • Provide personalized, professional guidance
  • Work within South African debt review regulations
